# DubFlow AI - Automated Multi-Speaker Video Dubbing SaaS

DubFlow AI is an end-to-end AI-powered video dubbing SaaS platform designed to automate:
- Multi-speaker audio detection & diarization
- Timed speech-to-text transcription
- Contextual neural translation preserving emotion and meaning
- Separate AI voice synthesis for every speaker
- Natural lip-sync & timing synchronization
- Background music & SFX preservation
- Cinema-grade video rendering
